Privacy Policy

App: Shadovv Notes (Android, com.shadovvdev.shadovvnotes)

Developer: Witchdoctor90

Contact: mrvnalexandr@gmail.com

Effective date: 15.10.2026

Shadovv Notes does not collect your notes or any personal data. Everything you write, draw or attach stays in the private storage of the app on your device. We run no servers for this app and there is no account.

  • Your content. Notes, photos, drawings, files and settings are stored only on your device, in the app’s private storage. The developer cannot see them and receives nothing.
  • Analytics, ads, trackers. None. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party tracking SDKs.
  • Network. The app asks for the Internet permission for one feature only: when you add a link card to a note, the app downloads that page directly from the website to read its title and description. This request goes from your device to the website you chose; it does not pass through any server of ours, and that website may see your IP address like with any visit. If you never add link cards, the app makes no network requests.
  • Photos, camera and files. When you add a photo, take a picture or attach a file, the app uses the system camera and the system picker. It receives only the items you choose and does not request broad access to your photos or storage.
  • Locked notes. If you lock a note, the app uses the system biometric or screen-lock prompt. It never receives or stores your fingerprint, face data or PIN; the note is encrypted with a key kept in the Android Keystore of your device.
  • Export and sharing. Notes leave the device only when you choose to export or share them, and only to the app or location you pick.
  • Backups. Android may include app data in the system backup if you have backups turned on in your device settings. Such backups are handled by your device and your Google account, not by us, and you can turn them off in your device settings.
  • Permissions. Internet (link previews, see above). The widgets, the quick-note window and the Share / Process text entries only pass what you send to them into the app.
  • Children. The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.
  • Deleting your data. Delete notes inside the app, or uninstall the app (or clear its storage in Android settings) to remove all of its data from your device.
  • Changes. If this ever changes, the new version of the policy will be published at this address with a new date.
